Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-18

Nolan Net Worth Tracker is a private application operated by Nolan Chu for personal net worth tracking. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may collect, how that information is used, how it is protected, and how it may be deleted.

Information collected

  • Account balances and current account values.
  • Basic account metadata such as institution name, account type, and masked or non-sensitive account descriptors.
  • Historical snapshot records created to measure net worth over time.
  • Authentication and connection artifacts supplied by linked-data providers, such as access tokens or refresh artifacts, where needed to maintain a connection.
  • Operational logs needed to debug and secure the application.

How information is used

  • To retrieve balance and investment-value information from linked accounts.
  • To compute total net worth, category allocation, and historical changes over time.
  • To maintain secure connectivity with linked data providers.
  • To troubleshoot errors, improve reliability, and protect the application from misuse.

How information is shared

Information is not sold. Information is not shared with third parties for advertising. Information may be processed by service providers that support the application, such as connected-account providers, hosting platforms, and source-control or automation services, solely for the purpose of operating the application.

Security

The application uses technical and administrative controls intended to protect information, including encryption in transit, access restrictions, multi-factor authentication for administrative accounts, secret management, and documented security practices. Additional details are available on the Security Overview page.

Retention and deletion

The application stores only the information needed to operate a historical net worth ledger and maintain connected-account access. For more detail, see the Data Retention and Deletion Policy.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the application, legal requirements, or security practices. The date at the top of the page reflects the latest revision.
