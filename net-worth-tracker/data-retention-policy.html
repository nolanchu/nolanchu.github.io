Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-18

This policy describes how Nolan Net Worth Tracker retains and deletes the limited financial and operational data used by the application.

Retention principles

  • Only the minimum data needed for net worth tracking and secure operation is retained.
  • Historical snapshot data is retained to preserve the accuracy of long-term net worth charts and change calculations.
  • Access tokens, secrets, and connection artifacts are retained only while an active linked-account connection is needed.

Typical retention categories

Historical snapshot data
Retained until deleted by the operator or until the application is discontinued, because the purpose of the application is to preserve historical net worth records.
Provider access tokens and related secrets
Retained only while needed to maintain connected-account access. Tokens are deleted or rotated when access is revoked, when credentials are replaced, or when compromise is suspected.
Operational logs
Retained only as long as reasonably necessary for troubleshooting, monitoring, and security review.
Backups or repository history
Where data is captured in backups or version history, removal from active storage may occur before removal from backup media or historical copies, which are removed through normal platform retention cycles.

Deletion process

  • Connected-account access artifacts can be removed when a connection is revoked or when the application is retired.
  • Stored snapshot data can be deleted from active storage by the operator.
  • Credentials and secrets are rotated promptly when exposure or misuse is suspected.
